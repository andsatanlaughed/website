A platform for men who refuse to let their worst moments write their final chapter.

There is a moment in every man’s life when he hits a wall.

A failure he can’t explain away.

A shame he can’t outrun.

A dream he has quietly stopped believing in.

And somewhere in that moment —

Satan laughed.

This platform exists because that laugh is not the end of the story.

. . . And Satan Laughed! LLC exists to inform, empower, and mobilize men who have been marginalized by events or circumstances in their past — to engage the culture, impact the world, and share their own HIStory.

This is not a platform for perfect men.

It is a platform for real ones.

Because here is what most men forget in the middle of their worst moment —

Satan was not the only one in the room.

About Dan

Dan — known to his grandchildren as Gramps and to his readers as Dr. NAD — is a retired Pastor, Hospital Chaplain, and Hospice Chaplain with more than four decades of ministry across six states.

Before any of that, he drove a forklift in a 100° diecast shop wearing a hardhat with no air-conditioning — which is where he learned that the common man carries uncommon weight.

A student of life more than academia, Dan has pursued formal education across seven colleges and universities while earning his real degree on factory floors, in hospital corridors, and at bedsides where time had run out.

He shares a daily Growth Concept across fifteen social media platforms and writes with a voice he calls GUS — Gramps’ Unique Style — built for the man who hasn’t willingly read a book since high school.

He and his wife Kathy have been married since 1979. They have two adult children, six grandchildren, and two children in heaven.

He is a fellow traveler —

beaten up by the road,

still walking,

and absolutely convinced that God has not finished with any of us yet.
